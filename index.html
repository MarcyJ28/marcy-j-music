About Marcy J

Marcy J, born Marcia, is a Christian independent artist and songwriter whose music flows from a place of faith, transparency, and deep personal restoration. A natural storyteller with a heart for healing, she writes songs that speak to the weary, the searching, and the hopeful — blending scripture, soul, and honest testimony into every lyric.

Her debut single, “Rest,” introduced her as a refreshing new voice in Christian music. Inspired by Matthew 11:28, the song invites listeners to lay down their burdens and find peace in God’s presence.

In 2026, she expanded her creative vision with the release of her full-length album, Elements of Me — a deeply personal journey from brokenness to restoration.
